Bangle Size Guide

Know your perfect size in just a few minutes.

How to Measure

Follow these simple methods to determine the correct bangle size for a comfortable and secure fit.

📏 1. Measure an Existing Bangle

If you already own a bangle that fits comfortably, this is the easiest and most accurate method.

  • Place the bangle on a flat surface.
  • Measure the inside diameter using a ruler.
  • Compare the measurement with the size chart below.

🖐 2. Wrap & Measure Your Hand

Bangles are NON-STRETCHABLE, so measuring your hand correctly is important.

  • Bring your thumb and little finger together.
  • Wrap a measuring tape around the widest part of your hand.
  • If using thread, measure its length with a ruler.
  • Compare the circumference with the size chart.

📐 3. Find Your Size

Compare your measurements with the chart below. If your measurement falls between two sizes, choose the larger size for maximum comfort.

✋ Hand Shape Guide

Slender Hand

If your measurement is close to the lower limit, you may prefer one size smaller.

Average Hand

Your measured size should provide the best everyday fit.

Broad Hand

If your measurement falls between two sizes, choosing the next larger size is recommended.

✔ Check the Fit

A properly fitted bangle should slide over your hand with slight resistance without feeling too loose or causing discomfort.

  • • Slides comfortably over your knuckles.
  • • Doesn't rotate excessively on your wrist.
  • • Doesn't require excessive force to wear.

💡 Helpful Tips

  • • Measure your hand in the evening for the most accurate result.
  • • Avoid measuring immediately after exercise.
  • • Glass bangles cannot stretch.
  • • If you're between sizes, choose the larger size.
  • • Measuring an existing well-fitting bangle provides the most accurate result.

Still Unsure?

If your measurements fall between two sizes, we recommend choosing the larger size for a comfortable fit.
