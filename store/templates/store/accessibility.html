Accessibility

Website & Technology Accessibility Statement

Accessibility Assistance

We are committed to making Aarti Collection website accessible to all customers. If you experience any difficulty accessing any part of our website or require assistance with browsing products, placing an order, or obtaining information, please contact us.

We will make every reasonable effort to provide the information, product details, or assistance you need through an accessible communication method.

Our Commitment

We believe everyone should be able to enjoy a seamless shopping experience. Our goal is to create an inclusive website that is easy to navigate for all users, including those using assistive technologies such as screen readers, magnifiers, keyboard navigation, voice recognition software, or other accessibility tools.

Accessibility Improvements

We continuously review and improve our website to enhance usability and accessibility. As our website grows, we remain committed to implementing best practices that help ensure an inclusive online experience.

Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is an ongoing process. We regularly evaluate our website and continue making improvements wherever possible to better serve all visitors.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback regarding the accessibility of our website. If you encounter any accessibility barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us.
